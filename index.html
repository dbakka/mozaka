About Mozaka

Built in
Kampala.
Played at
2am.

Mozaka is an East African Afrohouse DJ duo. Built in Kampala, the duo makes Afrohouse aimed at the global stage.

Rooted in East African identity, built for the room that doesn't stop until sunrise. African by root, global by sound.

The long-term vision: scale the Mozaka model across Nairobi, South Africa, and beyond. One sprint, one city, one camp at a time. One mission: bring the African sound to the world and make people move.
