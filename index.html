The Duo

About Mozaka

Mozaka transforms East Africa's native languages into peak-time dance music. Based in Kampala, Uganda, the Afrohouse duo writes hooks in Luganda, Runyankole, Acholi, Ateso, and Swahili, setting them over driving 3-step drums and deep, resonant house grooves.

From club anthems like "Buri Izooba" and "Neiwe" (with Café Riddim, Amanya, and DJ Spinny) to international collaborations with Turkish producer Kadnes ("What's There Not to Love About Here?"), Mozaka connects local soundscapes to the global electronic stage.

Supported across Apple Music (New Music Daily, EA Wave) and Spotify (AfroTech Now), a Mozaka live set is raw, energetic, and built for the dance floor.

The Wobuda Network

Wobuda (a Shona word meaning "you come out") is an opportunity for our fans and listeners to come out and join the Mozaka universe. Building the future of East African electronic music together.
